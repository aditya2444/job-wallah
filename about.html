Our Story & Purpose

Every year, over 15 million students graduate from colleges across India. However, for decades, top-tier corporate recruitment has remained confined to a handful of elite metropolitan institutes. Students from tier-2 and tier-3 towns, despite possessing world-class coding ability, creative talent, and relentless hunger, struggled to get even an initial interview call.

Job Wallah was established with a singular mission: to make the hiring ecosystem completely merit-driven, transparent, and accessible to every Indian youth. We connect aspiring interns and freshers directly with hiring managers at India’s fastest-growing startups and Fortune 500 enterprises.

Today, whether a student is studying in INDORE, COIMBATORE, JAIPUR, PATNA, or DELHI, Job Wallah ensures equal access to verified work-from-home internships and high-growth fresher roles with verified stipends and Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).

Our Core Pillars

100% Verified Openings

Every single posting is vetted with government registry (GSTIN/CIN) and work email checks to ensure complete safety and zero scam fee guarantees.

Guaranteed Fair Stipends

We reject unpaid internships. Top talent deserves respectful pay, career mentors, and pre-placement offers (PPOs) upon successful completion.

Skills Over Pedigree

We believe your real-world Github projects, design portfolio, and practical aptitude are 10x more important than your college tier or GPA.
