The Job Wallah Verification Shield

Students often encounter online job portals infested with counterfeit listings, identity theft, or deceptive "training programs" that ask candidates to pay money upfront.

At Job Wallah, student safety is our highest priority. Before any company or HR representative is permitted to publish an opening or review student resumes, they must successfully pass our comprehensive 4-Step Verification Protocol.

Our 4-Step Verification Process

1

Government Registration Cross-Check (GSTIN / CIN)

We cross-reference every organization's Goods & Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) or Corporate Identification Number (CIN) directly with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) database. Unregistered sole proprietorships or shell entities cannot post corporate roles.

2

Official Corporate Domain Authentication

Recruiters must register using their official company domain email (e.g. recruiter@razorpay.com or hr@swiggy.in). Generic free email accounts (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) are strictly rejected for enterprise job postings.

3

Fair Pay & Stipend Guarantee Policy

Job Wallah enforces a strict No Unpaid Internships rule. Employers must commit in writing to the agreed stipend range, start date, and certification perks before listings go live.

4

Recruiter Phone & Identity Verification

Our Trust & Safety team conducts telephonic screening calls with the hiring team, reviews public LinkedIn executive profiles, and validates physical office presence before awarding the ⚡ Verified Recruiter Badge.

Strict Zero-Tolerance Anti-Scam Policies

If an employer ever requests any of the following, they are in direct violation of Job Wallah terms:

  • ❌ Asking for Security Deposits: Demanding money for "laptops", "security clearance", or "uniforms".
  • ❌ Charging Training or Registration Fees: Forcing students to buy a course before giving an internship offer.
  • ❌ Telegram / Crypto Task Scams: Offering money for liking YouTube videos or completing unauthorized financial transfers.

Any employer found violating these guidelines is immediately permanently banned, and their incident report is submitted to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).
