Privacy Policy

Your trust is our cornerstone. We are committed to complete transparency in how your candidate profile and resumes are protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Job Wallah, we collect information necessary to connect you with verified employment opportunities:

  • Candidate Account Details: Name, email address, mobile number, current city, academic background, degree, graduation batch, and skills.
  • Application Materials: Resumes (PDF/DOCX), portfolios, GitHub/Behance links, and responses to screening questions.
  • Employer Information: Corporate email, MCA registration documents (GSTIN/CIN), organization size, and hiring contact details.
  • Technical Logs: IP address, browser type, and interaction timestamps to prevent bot attacks and fraudulent applications.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to power your job search and placement workflow:

  • Matching candidate profiles to relevant internships and fresher job openings.
  • Delivering applications directly to authorized hiring panels of companies you choose to apply to.
  • Sending critical status updates (Interview scheduled, Application Shortlisted, Recruiter response).
  • Preventing malicious bot activities and ensuring compliance with platform verification standards.

3. Resume Privacy & Recruiter Visibility Controls

Unlike open resume banks that expose candidate phone numbers to public scrapers, Job Wallah gives candidates granular privacy controls:

  • Explicit Application Sharing: Your resume and contact number are only released to a recruiter after you explicitly submit an application to their listing.
  • Recruiter Identity Validation: Only recruiters with verified GSTIN/CIN credentials can access candidate submission sheets.

4. Strict "Zero Third-Party Selling" Guarantee

Our Promise to You: Job Wallah never sells, rents, or monetizes student personal contact numbers or emails to third-party telemarketers, coaching institutes, or loan aggregators.

We believe student data is sacred. You will never receive unsolicited marketing calls or spam SMS from external coaching centers because of Job Wallah.

5. Data Encryption & Security Architecture

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • All web traffic is encrypted end-to-end using TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Stored resumes and sensitive candidate documents are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Quarterly third-party vulnerability assessments and penetration tests (VAPT).

6. Your Rights & Account Erasure

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, candidates maintain absolute control:

  • Access & Export: Request a complete machine-readable copy of your profile and application history.
  • Right to be Forgotten: You can delete your Job Wallah account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated resumes and contact records are permanently expunged from active databases within 7 days.
